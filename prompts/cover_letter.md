Write a cover letter in the SAME LANGUAGE as the vacancy text (Russian vacancy → Russian letter, English vacancy → English letter).

Rules (follow strictly):
- 500–750 characters with spaces — aim for this range; closer to 600 is ideal.
- Start in the middle of the matter, with something only this candidate could say: no greeting, no self-introduction, no statement of what you do or how long you have done it. A first sentence that would sit just as well in another candidate's letter is the wrong first sentence.
- Say it in your own words, not the posting's. This bans borrowed wording, not the subject: name plainly the specific thing this posting is about.
- Only facts from the candidate profile. Where the profile is silent about something the vacancy asks for, the letter is silent too.
- Personal voice — a person writing, not a list of bullet points and not a job description read back.
- Write е instead of ё throughout. Use a plain hyphen (-) instead of any dash (— or –) for all punctuation dashes.

Evidence: decide this once, before writing anything, and hold it for the whole letter. Does the profile state concrete metrics, meaning numbers tied to an outcome?
- It does → every claim rests on one. Never invent a number, never round one up, never turn a duty into a figure.
- It does not → this is an ordinary profile, not a deficient one; most work in the world is not counted. Do not invent, imply, or gesture at a number, and do not apologise for their absence. Rest claims on specifics of a different kind: scope (how many, how often, for whom), the actual situation handled, what changed because of it, named tools, environments or standards. Read "metric" below as "specific" throughout.
You are in one branch or the other for the entire letter — never half in each.

What opens the letter — decided here, and nowhere else in this prompt.
Find the case with the most genuine proximity to this vacancy. Genuine proximity means at least one of: the same field of work; the same thing at the centre of it (what is made, handled, treated, taught, sold, repaired or served); the same people on the other side of it; the same problem being solved (making something faster, safer, cheaper or larger; keeping it within rules; fixing what keeps breaking; carrying a load nobody else could). Closeness of the actual WORK beats closeness of the employer — the same industry with a different job is not proximity.
- Every section counts equally: employment, education, credentials, projects, publications, volunteering, awards. Equal by section, not by age — where two cases are comparably close the more recent one opens, and a case that ended many years ago opens only when nothing more recent comes near the work described.
- Comparable proximity is broken by THIS posting, never by the profile: take the case that speaks to the difficulty this posting describes. The case carrying the biggest numbers is not the answer — that ranks the candidate against themselves and writes the same opening for every employer.
- A qualifying case with metrics → open with it: one sentence, what was done and its most relevant metric, then connect to this vacancy. A case with a URL may be referenced as verifiable work. Stay on what was done; the reader does not want how the machinery behind it was arranged.
- No case has genuine proximity, or the proximate one has no metrics → do not force an unrelated case. Open instead with a specific observation about a real difficulty in this kind of work — it must name a thing that happens: a step, a constraint, a way it goes wrong, never a vague category or trend — then the method that transfers, then your nearest metric as execution evidence only.

Structure:
1. The opening decided above.
2. Back it with 1–2 pieces of evidence from the profile, in whichever branch you chose.
3. Close with one sentence that shows you have already thought about a real difficulty in this work — an observation, a hypothesis, or an angle you find interesting. It has to be about THIS employer's work as the posting describes it, and it must survive the test that makes it worth reading: it should be false, or at least useless, if pasted into a posting for a different job. Not a question asking what the role involves. Not "I look forward to hearing from you."

Output only the cover letter text, nothing else.

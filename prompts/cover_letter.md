Write a cover letter in the SAME LANGUAGE as the vacancy text (Russian vacancy → Russian letter, English vacancy → English letter).

Rules (follow strictly):
- 500–750 characters with spaces — aim for this range; closer to 600 is ideal.
- No greeting ("Добрый день", "Dear Hiring Manager", etc.)
- No template openings ("Меня заинтересовала вакансия...", "I would love to join...", "I am excited to apply...")
- Do not open with role, domain, or career summary — this includes ANY sentence starting with "Мой опыт" ("Мой опыт в...", "Мой опыт строился...", "Мой опыт включает...", "Мой опыт охватывает...", "Мой опыт связан..."), "За N лет в...", "I have N years of experience...", "Я занимаюсь..."
- Do NOT copy or paraphrase wording from the vacancy — use your own language
- Only use facts from the candidate profile — never invent
- If the profile says nothing about something the vacancy asks for, say nothing about it
- Personal voice — not a list of bullet points, not a job description recitation
- Write е instead of ё throughout. Use a plain hyphen (-) instead of any dash (— or –) for all punctuation dashes.

Evidence: decide this once, before writing anything, and hold it for the whole letter.
Read the candidate profile and answer one question — does it state concrete metrics, meaning
numbers tied to an outcome?
- It does → every claim rests on one. Open with a metric, close with a metric. Never invent a
  number, never round one up, never turn a duty into a figure.
- It does not → this is an ordinary profile, not a deficient one; most work in the world is not
  counted. Do not invent, imply, or gesture at a number, and do not apologise for their absence.
  Rest claims on specifics of a different kind: scope (how many, how often, for whom), the actual
  situation handled, what changed because of it, named tools, environments or standards. For you,
  every instruction below that says "metric" reads as "specific".
You are in one branch or the other for the entire letter — never half in each.

Case selection: before writing, identify which of the candidate's cases (professional or side projects) has the most genuine domain proximity to this vacancy. Genuine proximity means at least one of: same domain, same product mechanics (B2C storefront, compliance layer, internal ops tooling, consumer AI, etc.), same user type (B2C consumer, B2B enterprise, regulated users), or same problem pattern (reduce CAC, grow LTV, cut TTR, ensure compliance). If ≥1 case qualifies and has concrete metrics: open with that case and its most relevant metric. If no case has genuine proximity — or the proximate case has no concrete metrics — do NOT force an unrelated case: open instead with a specific observation about a real tension or challenge in this vacancy's domain (the observation must name a specific mechanism, metric, or user behavior — not a vague category or trend), then demonstrate transferable methodology, then close with your nearest metric as execution evidence only.

Proximity shortcuts: evaluate ALL of the candidate's evidence with equal priority regardless of which section it sits in — employment, education, credentials, projects, publications, volunteering, awards. Select the case with highest genuine domain proximity — topical/mechanic relevance to the vacancy beats organizational relevance (same employer/industry but different actual subject matter). If the candidate profile states a career aspiration (Career Profile → aspiration) and this vacancy's domain supports that direction, prefer a case that evidences it — even one with fewer or softer metrics — over a more metric-dense case that doesn't support the stated direction. If multiple cases have comparable proximity and no aspiration applies, prefer the one with more concrete metrics. If a case has a URL, you may reference it as verifiable work. Opener: one sentence (what + one metric), then connect to the vacancy. Do not expand on architecture or pipeline.

Structure:
1. Open with the most specific point of intersection between the candidate's experience and this vacancy — not with job title, not with years of experience
2. Back it with 1–2 pieces of evidence from the profile, in whichever branch you chose above
3. Close with one sentence that shows you have already thought about a real challenge or tension in this product/domain — an observation, a hypothesis, or an angle you find interesting. NOT a question asking what the role involves. NOT "I look forward to hearing from you."

Output only the cover letter text, nothing else.
